Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Infyra News (hereinafter "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses and protects your personal information when using our website.

1. What data we collect

When you visit our site, the following data is automatically collected:

  • Cookies — small text files stored on your device containing information about your preferences and actions on the site;
  • Anonymized visit data — traffic sources, session duration, pages viewed (using web analytics services);
  • Technical information — device type, browser version, IP address (without personal identification);
  • Form data — if you contact us through the contact form, we collect your name and email.

2. How we use your data

The collected data is used exclusively for:

  • Ensuring the operation and improving the functionality of the site;
  • Analyzing user behavior to optimize content;
  • Personalizing advertising (via Google AdSense and other advertising partners);
  • Statistical and marketing analysis of the audience;
  • Responding to your requests through the contact form.

3. Cookies

We use cookies for:

  • Ensuring the functioning of the site (technical cookies);
  • Collecting statistics (analytical cookies);
  • Displaying personalized advertising (marketing cookies).

You can change your cookie settings in your browser at any time or disable them. By continuing to use our site, you consent to the use of cookies.

4. Google AdSense and advertising

Our site uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google may use cookies to display personalized ads based on your previous visits. You can disable ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

5. Transfer of data to third parties

We do not sell or transfer your personal data to third parties, except for the use of web analytics services and advertising partners (Google AdSense), who process data in accordance with their privacy policies.

6. Data protection

We take necessary technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, leakage or alteration.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Disable cookies in your browser settings;
  • Request information about your personal data and request its deletion;
  • Opt out of personalized advertising in Google settings.

8. Contact information

For all questions related to data processing, you can contact us through the feedback form. We will respond as soon as possible.

9. Policy changes

We reserve the right to make changes to this Privacy Policy. The current version is always available on this page.
